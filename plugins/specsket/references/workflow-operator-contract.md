# Operator workflow contract

Read [the workflow contract](workflow-contract.md) first; this file adds the vendor identity, storefront, company-settings and existing-delete rules.

## Evidence on company settings

Company settings can carry field evidence only when capabilities report a research schema version that is actually enabled. If a settings validation answers `vendor_research_disabled`, use the plain settings shape, tell the user the field evidence was not stored, and record it for a later pass; do not assume the advertised research shape works.

## Existing-delete custody reconciliation

This operator cleanup is separate from ordinary ingestion, incomplete-draft recovery and ingestion completion. Use `specsket_reconcile_existing_deleted_product_chunk` only for the explicitly reviewed original operation when `specsket_get_capabilities` reports `product_chunk_zero_effect_custody.enabled: true` and its `reconcile_existing_deleted_operation` names that tool, the principal is `platform_admin` with a validated `current_operator_binding`, and a current finite grant authorizes that exact operation under the same user, OAuth client, integration authorization and epoch. The recovery tool, advertised `status_operation` (`specsket_get_product_chunk_zero_effect_custody`), and `specsket_get_ingestion_job` must all be callable in this chat. Missing tools or grants are a connection or authorization limitation; stop and report it through the governed operator process. Capability advertisement, a historical receipt and an old grant do not authorize execution.

Use only the reviewed exact `operation_id`, `callerId`, `claimGeneration`, `expectedJobUpdatedAt`, `stageId`, `sha256`, `byteLength` and `deleteJobId` accepted by the live tool schema. The call reconciles a completed existing native delete and does not request another delete, stage or dispatch. Preserve the original operation and immutable versions; do not invent a replacement identifier to bypass a stale claim or binding. After a response, reconnect or `read_exact_custody_status` direction, read `specsket_get_product_chunk_zero_effect_custody` with that same `operation_id` and read its exact original job through `specsket_get_ingestion_job`. `existing_delete_resolved` with the qualified existing-deletion result confirms terminal cleanup; report job state and accepted/rejected counts separately. If status is unavailable, expired, revoked or binding denied, the outcome remains `unknown`: stop for governed status and grant recovery. Never automatically redispatch this recovery, create a new key, revive old authorization or use a private API. A returned `prepare_fresh_reviewed_attempt` is a proposed next action, not permission to prepare, stage or resume business ingestion.

The existing-delete recovery schema requires an exact `stageId` and completed `deleteJobId`, matching the reviewed original stage bytes, hash, length, delete target and immutable job/claim binding. A zero native-object count, a delete request or a different completed delete is not completed exact delete proof. For a no-stage original, do not invent either identifier or use this recovery; apply only the canonical zero-stage closure conjunction in [the workflow contract](workflow-contract.md#canonical-zero-stage-closure).

Complete-set custody recovery is a separate platform-operator cleanup, not part of ordinary ingestion or its recovery. Its tools (`specsket_request_product_chunk_custody_set`, `specsket_get_product_chunk_custody_set_status`, `specsket_finalize_product_chunk_custody_set`) exist only for a `platform_admin` with a validated `current_operator_binding` when `specsket_get_capabilities` reports `product_chunk_custody_set.enabled: true` and names them as its operations; use them only for an explicitly reviewed original operation under a current grant, and otherwise treat set recovery as unavailable. Do not offer set recovery to anyone else, fabricate a delete-set receipt, request automatic deletion or interpret this proposal as permission to close a staged original. A current staged original remains `unknown` until its own supported authoritative no-commit, complete custody and quiescence proof exists under current bindings. One older zero-stage or existing-delete closure does not resolve it.

## Vendor identity and storefront reviews

Call specsket_get_capabilities and inspect the current callable tools first. A capability flag is not proof that this chat can invoke its operation. If refreshed tools are absent, follow the connection's supported metadata refresh and start a new conversation; do not substitute privileged APIs or guessed tool calls. Apply ordinary OAuth role, exact vendor, separate product-ingestion or membership permission, and rollout-scope checks. A vendor sign-up may already provision its company; read current context before proposing creation. Never infer account ownership from a brand, domain or manufacturer.

For platform-admin onboarding, use specsket_resolve_product_vendor and the live onboarding contract. Preserve authoritative country, registrable-domain identity and the resolver digest. Same or unknown market domain collisions block new creation; known distinct country operators may share a domain when the live resolver and reviewed evidence permit it. Name similarity alone does not establish a duplicate. Validate and show exact identity, matches, warnings and evidence, then confirm staging through specsket_stage_vendor_onboarding_proposal. Staging stores a proposal only.

When create_vendor_via_mcp is enabled and specsket_create_vendor_from_onboarding_review is callable, show the exact owned create-new review revision and digest for explicit confirmation. Creation returns one pending platform-managed vendor; it does not create a login, activate, claim or publish. Recover its exact receipt before retrying. Activation requires its separate enabled/callable specsket_activate_vendor_from_onboarding_review, explicit effect, reason, review binding and stable idempotency key. Existing companies must be selected by exact permitted ID rather than recreated.

For settings and storefront edits, read specsket_read_vendor_storefront (context, draft and readiness by default; add contract once for the change-set shapes). Canonical country/name/domain/contact settings use their own timestamp-bound validate/stage/apply workflow and change live company identity. Storefront overrides use explicit set/clear/add/update/remove/complete-list reorder operations bound to draft revision and digest. Omitted fields remain unchanged. Propose the change set with specsket_propose_vendor_storefront_changes, which validates and stages it in one call, read the staged review with specsket_get_vendor_review and confirm the exact staged diff before specsket_confirm_vendor_review (kind storefront_change) when draft_apply_via_mcp is enabled; this changes only the private draft. If apply is unavailable, return the permanent native review URL. Protect certification-review fields according to the current role.

When vendor_media_contract is enabled and tools are callable, use specsket_stage_vendor_image for the exact company logo, cover, collection, saved gallery row or saved resource row. Show source and target before upload confirmation, then read and separately confirm the immutable owned media review before specsket_confirm_vendor_review (kind image). Preserve PNG/JPEG/WebP versus PDF slot requirements and native byte/size/ownership validation. Do not turn source URLs into invented native ownership. Media adoption does not publish a storefront.

For vendor products, fetch specsket_get_vendor_product_authoring_contract and read any existing exact product. Prepare save_product or publish_product through specsket_prepare_vendor_product_change, show the immutable proposal and confirm it with specsket_confirm_vendor_review (kind product_change). Preserve original custody, source/manufacturer/market evidence, variants, technical occurrences and document applicability. New saves are private drafts; product publication is separate. Use specsket_import_vendor_product_media only for its exact saved product and permitted slot. Product-ingestion candidates retain their existing human-review path; authoring does not silently approve them or change their destinations.

Before storefront publication, read readiness with specsket_read_vendor_storefront. Show exact activation/media/product blockers separately from recommendations and unverified remote decoding. Prepare specsket_prepare_vendor_storefront_publication against current company timestamp plus draft revision/digest, read the returned review, display its public effect and obtain explicit confirmation before specsket_confirm_vendor_review with kind storefront_publication and publish true. Storefront publication does not publish products. Verify native preview and public visibility separately; return exact IDs, revisions, receipts and per-step errors without claiming unperformed browser/media checks.

After a lost response or reconnect, read specsket_get_vendor_authoring_operations and the exact review/receipt before repeating creation or publication. Historical IDs do not authorize mutation under an old grant. Retry exact keys/payloads only when current access permits; a changed draft/company requires reread and a fresh review. Never overwrite a newer edit to resolve a conflict.

If vendor_governance_contract.membership_enabled is true and the tools are callable, governance requires its independent explicit permission. Read exact company members/version; prepare and explicitly confirm only an existing member role change or removal. Recheck current access even on replay. No self-change, last-admin removal, account creation, invitation send or claim transfer is supported. Native claim handoffs require human proof and do not themselves transfer ownership.

Permanent review URLs remain authoritative after signed-in links expire. Short-lived signed-in review links must follow the separate account-switch confirmation rules in [the workflow contract](workflow-contract.md#visible-source-review). Never present an expired link or claim a browser opened without a successful browser action.

Platform administrators can rediscover both new-vendor onboarding and existing-storefront proposals in the single **Vendor Reviews** inbox at `/admin/products?section=imports&view=vendor-reviews` (with `/admin/product-import?tab=vendor-reviews` retained as an alias). Vendor administrators can rediscover authorized proposals for their own vendor under **Storefront > MCP Suggestions** at `/dashboard/vendor/storefront?tab=suggestions`. These inboxes are review surfaces, not MCP approval: product-ingestion candidate approval remains an in-app decision, and vendor-authoring publication is its own separately confirmed MCP operation when enabled.
