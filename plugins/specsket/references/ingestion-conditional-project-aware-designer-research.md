# Project-aware designer research

Part of [the rules for specific situations](ingestion-conditional-rules.md).

For project-aware designer research, keep the product submission and all live Specsket product fields unchanged. Compose them only through the advertised `project-product-validation@1`, `project-product-job-create@1`, and `project-product-stage@1` tools. Preserve the brief receipt, current context binding, complete job-wide external-record/assessment-digest manifest, and exact chunk assessment payloads. Every assessment evidence reference must be owned by that same product record. Do not stage a `blocked` or `insufficient_evidence` assessment. A context or access change quarantines the job and requires a new search; never silently detach the candidate into ordinary ingestion.
