# Company and storefront research completeness

Use this contract when researching or populating a company/storefront. Read the live authoring context and contract first. Use the returned field catalog and supported request schemas when research attestations are advertised. Do not send a proposed schema or invent an unavailable tool. On older servers, retain the same research discipline in the available field evidence and unresolved warnings, and report that durable field-level research tracking is unavailable.

## Default research

Research every relevant company and storefront field using official company pages, including contact/about pages, local operator pages, applications, strengths, sustainability, official social links, locations, collections and available media/resources/certifications. Record the actual pages checked and their outcome. Search results alone do not verify a company fact. A failed or inaccessible page is insufficient evidence, not proof that the value does not exist.

Fill verified facts within the user's scope. Leave a field blank only after research finds no clear value, it is not applicable, evidence conflicts, or disclosure is restricted; record the reason and sources. Never fill optional fields with samples, invented claims, or unrelated assets. An unresearched blank is unfinished. Preserve any user-requested research limits and report remaining fields instead of pretending they were researched.

Canonical company contacts and profile overrides have different purposes. Read the effective value and its origin. Verified canonical inheritance counts without duplicating contacts into profile overrides. A present canonical value without source evidence is present but not research-verified. Do not call a generic address headquarters unless the official source establishes that location type.

## Field records and saved state

When the server supports field research records, bind each record to the live field/item ID and value digest. Use its advertised outcomes for verified, not found, not applicable, conflicting, restricted or insufficient evidence. Missing records mean unresearched; changed values make prior evidence stale. Show exact values, source pages, blank reasons and unresolved findings in the owned immutable review. Read back both saved values and research records after an authorized apply.

The server enforces scope, digests and recorded coverage; an AI's attestation does not prove independent source verification. Attribute research honestly. Do not claim Specsket verified external truth unless an actual server verification result supports it.

## Separate completion claims

- Research reviewed: relevant fields have recorded, current research outcomes; report conflicts, restrictions and inaccessible sources.
- Population saved: verified values are saved or correctly inherited, with unresolved fields and delivery failures disclosed.
- Publication: separately authorized public effect and actual receipt/readback. Publication permission does not establish complete population.

Optional researched blanks need not prevent publication. Do not turn a research outcome into a new publication requirement or claim an overall completeness percentage without an explicit catalog and denominator.

Official image/document discovery is distinct from managed upload, adoption, decoding and public availability. Preserve exact source URLs and owned receipts. A storage gate or retained failed stage remains a delivery blocker even when its source was researched successfully. Do not recreate reviews, transfer their ownership, invent upload receipts or reset storage state to resolve that blocker.

Keep the requested product scope: one light display product requires its true name, short description, category, matching genuine image, official source URL and collection. Company profile research does not authorize a deeper product extraction or replacing existing rich products.
